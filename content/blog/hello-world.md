Welcome to the blog. Every post is a Markdown file in `content/blog/` with a
title, a date, its authors and a short description at the top. Galley offers
the names from `data/authors.yaml` when you pick the authors, and a new post
starts as a copy of the newest one beside it.

Links can open in a new tab, like this one to the
[Hugo documentation](https://gohugo.io/documentation/ "NewTab").
